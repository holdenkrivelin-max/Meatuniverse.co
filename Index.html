The Mission

The Simple Mission
Create community with likeminded people through food and conversation. People come. They're open. They leave better than they arrived.
Why It Started
I saw Thirty Love close — a beautiful place, gone, forgotten. I couldn't accept that. So from 6pm to midnight, with no budget and just an idea, I made MEAT real. Everything you see here grew from that hustle.
Why MEAT Universe Exists
That one person's mission is expanding into everything below. Dinners. A platform. An app. Rewards. Merch. Media. All built on the same belief: beautiful ideas shouldn't die in silence.

How We Get There

Prove the dinner format repeats. Earn real partnerships — starting with even one real MEAT Points redemption. Build the app, simply at first. Then grow from what's actually proven, not from a bigger pitch.

Relevance Before Inventory

Most rewards ecosystems show you every offer and hope something sticks. MEAT learns you first — what you love, what you don't — then only shows what actually fits your life. The right person, the right room, the right opportunity, the right reward. One user at a time.

MEAT Grows As You Grow

Most apps recommend the same thing forever. MEAT keeps asking: where are you, where would you like to be — and updates as the answers change. MEAT doesn't define you by who you were when you signed up. It evolves with who you're becoming.

There for the milestones, and everything in between.

If Life Gets in the Way

Yesterday's task doesn't just disappear. Keep it today, move it, break it smaller, mark it complete, or drop it. If you keep moving the same thing, MEAT can ask: "You've moved this three times. Want me to make it easier?" Accountability without shaming.

Not daily minutes spent in MEAT. Daily lives moved by MEAT.

Instagram says look at everybody else. TikTok says keep watching. MEAT says: "Hey, what are you up to today?" And then actually helps.

MEAT helps you remember your life, organize your life, and actually live it.
