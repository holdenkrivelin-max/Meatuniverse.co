Our Mission

MEAT is building the future of how people connect, grow, and experience hospitality.

We create experiences, develop operators, tell stories, and build technology that brings people together.

We never set out to be Meateocre. MEAT Hospitality Group was born from watching too many great operators and chefs risk their dreams, hoping people would show up. We set out to change that.

We exist to unify meaningful experiences under one company with one shared mission.

Every idea we build, every experience we create, and every step we take is guided by the same purpose: to bring people together, inspire them to take their first step, and grow alongside them.

We don't build disconnected concepts. We build one ecosystem where every part belongs to the same story.

Because when people grow, we grow.

We want the MEAT App to be something people of every age carry with them into their everyday decisions — and a reminder that we're all in this together when life feels heavy. The same way the shows and movies we grew up with stayed with us, we want everything MEAT puts out, now and in the years ahead, to be a guide people carry for a lifetime.

How MEAT Points Work +

Too many great experiences are gated behind a paywall — reserved for whoever can already afford them. We don't think access should work that way. It shouldn't matter who you know or what you can spend. It should matter that you're willing to take the first step.

That's why we're building Meat Points — earned not just by spending, but by living. Getting up and out of the house. Talking to someone new. Starting the thing you've been putting off. Asking the question you've been too nervous to ask. Every step forward earns you points, and points unlock access — a seat at a dinner, entry into new tiers, experiences that used to be reserved for whoever could already afford them. The reward for growing is more access to grow.

Every verified check-in earns 10 Meat Points — and if you complete it with a meatie, that jumps to 20. Show up for your life, and the points add up fast.

Just Keep Meating reminders send a quick nudge in the morning to get you excited for your tasks that day — "Good morning. Just Keep Meating — here are your reminders for today."

Those points live inside MEAT Rewards — and it's not just for MEAT events and dinners. Restaurants, cafes, bakeries, coffee shops, chef spotlights, unannounced concepts still in the works — even ground-floor preorder access to what's opening next. Why should you only get rewarded for doing one thing?

Preorder one of those ground-floor experiences and you'll see who else is waiting alongside you — building the hype before the place even opens its doors.

Gyms. Spas. Saunas. Day trips. The everyday things nobody else is rewarding you for. The bigger we grow, the more we'll add. Welcome to everyday living as it should be.

Here's how it works: do it on your own, or connect with a meatie who's doing something similar — complete the task, verify it happened, then move on to your next objective. No hollow self-checkboxes. Real steps, confirmed by real people.

We want to reward you for what you do every day, with things you already do. Went grocery shopping? That's life. Met a friend for coffee? Life. Went on a date, walked the dog, tried a new restaurant, worked on your idea, finally sent the email you've been avoiding? All life.

You don't necessarily have to do it alone. See what others are up to. Connect based on shared interest. Hold each other accountable. Complete the task together — or do it on your own. Verify each other's progress. Earn Meat Points. Keep moving. Either way, you earn points for taking that first step. We encourage you to go with how you feel and what you like — every activity is based on your interests. Someone should always be able to take the first step alone and still have it count — the connection is there for when you don't want to go it solo, not a requirement to earn access.

When you sign up, a short survey gets to know your interests — then we show you meaties who match your interests, so you can connect and share what you're up to or what you'd like to accomplish. Share, message, laugh, and connect. That's the Meat way.

Our goal is to create a Meatsperience you'll be talking about for years to come. Once you sign up, you're part of the Meat family — for life.

We're not rewarding spending. We're rewarding participation in life. Go somewhere. Do something. Meat someone. Take the first step. MEAT rewards you for participating — and Meat Points turn participation into possibility.

Every Decision We Make

The MEAT Test.

Does this help someone participate more in their life?

If yes, it may belong. If it only increases screen time, data collection, or engagement without helping someone act, connect, create, or experience something in the real world — it probably doesn't. Every feature we build has to earn its place against that one question.

MEAT is not trying to become the place where you spend your life.

MEAT is trying to become the platform that helps you participate in the life you already have — and build the parts you still want.

We didn't invent showing up for each other, or trying something new, or building the thing you've been putting off. That's always been possible. We just built something new that's been right in front of us the whole time.

Before Anything Else

Trust & Safety.

The app doesn't exist yet — but the standard it's held to already does. These are the commitments the app is being built around from day one, not features bolted on after something goes wrong.

Verified before you can match.

Phone verification is the floor for creating a profile — no anonymous accounts, no bots pretending to be people.

Public places, by default.

First-time meetups between people who don't know each other default to suggested public spots — never a private address.

Block and report, one tap away.

Reachable directly from any profile or conversation — never buried, never optional to find.

A real person reviewing reports.

Every report gets looked at by a human, not a queue that quietly disappears. Safety concerns get a same-day response.

Only what's needed, nothing more.

No workplace, no exact address, no background tracking. What we don't collect can't be a problem later.

A real bridge, not a replacement, for real help.

A meatie is a great accountability partner for a lot of things. For substance use, mental health, or recovery, MEAT will always point you toward real, trained support first — not a stranger from an app. SAMHSA's National Helpline (1-800-662-HELP) is free, confidential, and available 24/7.

This list will only get more specific as the app gets built — not less.

Not a Static App

Most apps recommend the same kind of thing forever. MEAT is built to keep asking three questions, and to keep updating as the answers change:

Where are you now?
What are you trying to become?

Finish law school, and MEAT shifts toward bar prep, networking, interviews, first-job goals. Launch the business, and it moves from "name it" and "talk to one customer" toward sales, systems, partnerships, hiring. Hit a fitness goal, and it stops feeding you beginner tasks — it moves with you.

And you choose how you want to move: hold yourself accountable, get matched with a meatie, or invite someone you already know. Either way, both people can see the shared objective, check progress, and encourage each other along the way.

MEAT is life-stage aware.

Personalization evolves as you evolve — so yesterday's first step doesn't become tomorrow's irrelevant recommendation.

That's why signing up with MEAT doesn't start with your age, your title, or your net worth. It starts with two questions:

Where are you?
Where would you like to be?

Our Story

9
Years watching great concepts disappear

It was a day like any other. I had just come from a conversation with my mom and brother about my trajectory in life — the kind of conversation where you're expected to just accept things as they are. Walking out afterward, right across the street, I saw it: 30 Love, this big, beautiful, majestic bar and grill, closed, dark, a "for lease" sign in the window.

That was the moment. I was done accepting what life was supposed to be. There had to be a way for operators, foodies, and chefs to build the next big hospitality idea together — before it ended up behind a "for lease" sign of its own. I called it MEAT. By that night, my entire life had changed.

But 30 Love was only the spark. MEAT is really made up of a few pieces. A piece of my dad — not with me now, but the one who taught me to be relentless, to just keep showing up even when I didn't feel like it. A piece of Stormi, my dog, who taught me the same thing a different way: build trust over fear, one step at a time. And a piece of me — wanting to connect with somebody on a real level, and on top of that, wanting to do it with somebody else. To feel like I'm not alone.

I think that's why this resonates past just restaurants. It's not really about the industry. It's about showing up anyway.

"I wanted to create a place where chefs and foodies and operators can submit their concepts and feel like they have something. I want to change the way ideas are brought to life."

— Holden Krivelin, Founder & CEO · MEAT Hospitality Group

Investor Overview

MEAT Hospitality Group

MEAT Hospitality Group is an early-stage hospitality and brand company based in New York City. The company operates a portfolio of connected businesses — intimate chef-led dinner experiences, a direct-to-consumer merchandise line, and a media/content arm — unified under a single brand and a shared points-based rewards system currently in development.

The Model

Revenue and audience are built across four connected lines rather than a single product:

  • Dinners — ticketed, chef-led events; the core product and primary trust-builder with guests
  • Merchandise — direct-to-consumer apparel and goods sold through the company's Shopify storefront
  • Media — a newsletter, podcast, and content presence used for audience growth and retention at low marginal cost
  • Community/App — a rewards and social-connection app in early development, intended to unify engagement across all of the above
